mini-kvstore

Python | Raft consensus | WAL | TCP | ~1500 lines | 2026.07

What it does

A distributed key-value store with Raft-based consensus. Multiple nodes form a cluster, elect a leader, and replicate writes across nodes.

Three-layer architecture

Store (store.py): in-memory dict + WAL, JSON-line format,
  snapshot compaction, crash recovery

Network (net.py): custom protocol [4-byte len][JSON],
  TCP with auto-reconnection

Consensus (raft.py): leader election, log replication,
  commit only current-term entries (5.4.2)

Raft: Leader election

Follower/Candidate/Leader states. Randomized timeout 3-6s. On timeout: increment term, vote for self, RequestVote. Win with majority. Step down on higher term.

Raft: Log replication

Leader appends to log, sends AppendEntries. On majority ACK, commit and apply. Tracks nextIndex/matchIndex per follower.

Raft: Safety

  1. Election restriction: candidate log >= majority
  2. Commit only current-term entries
  3. Term never decreases

WAL

{"op": "set", "key": "name", "val": "tom"}
{"op": "del", "key": "temp"}

Problems

  • Election split at 100ms timeout, fixed at 3-6s
  • Stale leader: added term checks to every RPC handler
  • Pickle WAL undebuggable, switched to JSON lines
